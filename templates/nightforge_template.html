Components such as the portrait, body sections, arms, hands, accessories, effects and display-base elements may remain separate depending on the original model geometry.

The model should be primed using a suitable miniature or model primer before painting.

Cleaning & Preparation

Every model is professionally cleaned, prepared and fully UV cured before dispatch.

Approximately 95% of printing supports are removed during production; however, some micro-support contact points, small support remnants or minor surface marks may remain.

Buyers should expect normal hobby preparation including light cleanup, minor sanding and test fitting before assembly, priming and painting.

Any remaining supports on the cured resin should be carefully removed using flush cutters or a sharp hobby knife. Cut close to the surface rather than pulling or snapping supports away, as this can damage fine details.

Remaining support marks can be gently smoothed using fine-grit sandpaper or sanding sticks, approximately 400–800 grit.

Avoid breathing resin dust when sanding and use appropriate protective equipment.
